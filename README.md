<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📊 KPI Cards
MTD and YTD accounts with LC / LG and CA / SA split, EOP and AMB balance, and average per account. Every card has a **trend sparkline** and month-on-month change.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🏅 Best Performers
The top RM by **accounts** and by **balance**, for both the current month and the year, surfaced at a glance.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📈 7 Ready Charts
Monthly trend · Branch vs Channel · Top 10 RMs (accounts and balance) · Quarterly performance · LC vs LG mix · Product mix. Each has an **MTD / YTD toggle** and a **chart-type switch**.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🎛️ Smart Filters
Region · Circle · Channel · Product · LC / LG · Report month · EOP / AMB · **Calendar or Financial Year (Apr–Mar)** basis.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🛠️ Build Your Own
**Add New Chart** with any dimension and measure, and **Calculated Fields** to create your own metrics with a simple formula. No coding.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🧮 Pivot and 🏆 Leaderboard
A flexible pivot table (Sum or Average) and a searchable RM leaderboard with **Gold, Silver and Bronze** ranking across MTD, YTD or all data.

</td>
